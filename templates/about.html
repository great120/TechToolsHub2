About Tech ToolsHub

Our Mission

At Tech ToolsHub, our mission is to provide free, high-quality online tools that help people solve everyday problems efficiently. We believe that useful technology should be accessible to everyone, which is why all our tools are completely free to use without any registration requirements.

What We Offer

Tech ToolsHub offers a comprehensive suite of online utilities, including:

  • QR Code Generator for creating scannable codes for any text or URL
  • Text Extraction from images and PDFs using OCR technology
  • Scientific Calculator for complex mathematical operations
  • Date Difference Calculator to find the time between two dates
  • BMI Calculator to check if you're in a healthy weight range
  • Age Calculator that determines your exact age from your birth date
  • Percentage Calculator for financial calculations and statistics
  • Unit Converter for transforming between different measurement systems

Our Values

We are guided by these core principles:

  • Accessibility: All tools should be easy to use and available to everyone.
  • Privacy: We respect your data and don't collect unnecessary information.
  • Quality: We strive to provide accurate, reliable, and useful tools.
  • Simplicity: Our interfaces are designed to be clean and intuitive.
